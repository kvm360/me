Inside the EHVT-75

One Platform, Three Diagnostic Panels

01 — All-In-One Testing

Compact Hematology, Urine & Feces Testing

EHVT-75 integrates blood, urine and feces test modules into one device, saving lab bench space and letting a single technician run a full routine panel without switching machines mid-workup.

02 — AI-Powered Precision

Morphology Evidence, Not Just a Flag

Powered by Complete Blood Morphology (CBM) analysis, EHVT-75 identifies abnormal cells such as RET, SPH, ETG, APLT and P-LCC and attaches a captured image to the result — giving the vet visual evidence behind every abnormal number.

03 — Maintenance-Free by Design

Built for Space-Constrained Clinics

A no-fluid-path architecture and single-use reagent cartridges remove daily internal cleaning and cross-contamination risk. No cold-chain storage is required, which keeps logistics simple across Indian clinics outside major metros too.

What Makes It Different

Built for Same-Visit, First-Line Diagnostics

9-Diff CBM Hematology

48 hematology parameters with Complete Blood Morphology analysis — well beyond a standard 3- or 5-part CBC — surfacing cell abnormalities most basic analyzers miss.

29-Parameter Urinalysis

Covers casts, cells, crystals, microorganisms and other markers directly on the same instrument — no separate urine analyzer needed.

29-Parameter Fecal Analysis

Parasite eggs, digestive-function markers, cells, microorganisms and intestinal protozoa — replacing manual fecal microscopy for routine workups.

AI-Assisted Open Dx Reports

Structures results into clear reports with conversational AI-assisted context on treatment history and medication guidance — support for the vet's decision, not a replacement for it.

10.1-Inch Touchscreen

A single full-colour interface runs all three panels, keeping training time low for clinic staff who are new to in-house diagnostics.

No-Fluid-Path Cartridges

Single-use reagent cartridges with dry QC card calibration mean no daily cleaning cycle and no cold-chain reagent storage to manage.

LIS & USB Connectivity

Connects into existing practice management or LIS software over LAN or USB, so results flow straight into the patient record.

10,000-Result On-Device Storage

Common Questions

What Indian Clinics Ask About the EHVT-75

What tests does the EHVT-75 perform?

It runs three panels from one machine: a 48-parameter 9-diff CBM hematology panel on blood, a 29-parameter urinalysis panel, and a 29-parameter fecal analysis panel covering parasites, digestive function, cells and microorganisms.

Can one analyzer really replace separate blood, urine and feces machines?

Yes — the EHVT-75 is built as a multi-functional platform specifically so a clinic doesn't need three separate instruments. All three sample types run through the same no-fluid-path cartridge system and touchscreen interface.

How much does a veterinary hematology analyzer cost in India?

Basic 3-part veterinary hematology analyzers in India list from roughly ₹1.9–3 lakh, with 5-part systems and government e-marketplace listings for established brands priced higher. Multi-functional analyzers with morphology imaging and urine/feces capability, like the EHVT-75, are priced above single-function units — contact Stethix Healthcare for a current India quote based on your clinic's testing volume.

Does the EHVT-75 need cold storage for reagents or regular maintenance?

No. It uses single-use reagent cartridges that don't require cold-chain storage, and its no-fluid-path design removes the need for daily internal cleaning — maintenance is limited to routine cartridge changes and automatic calibration.

Is the EHVT-75 suitable for a small, single-doctor clinic?

Yes. At 8 kg and a compact footprint, it's sized for clinics with limited bench space, and its throughput of up to 6 CBM samples per hour matches the daily caseload of a general small-animal practice rather than a high-volume referral lab.

What species does it support?

Canine and feline. Ozelle has indicated additional species and customization options are planned — ask your Stethix representative for the current roadmap.
